# Community Event Outreach

Start from the current event fact sheet, audience goals, existing outreach records, channel access, and the requested task. Confirm the official date, local time zone, venue, registration link, host and sponsor wording, audience fit, and approved benefits before reusing copy. If records conflict, resolve them against the event owner’s latest decision or the relevant primary source. Call out material unknowns in the draft.

## Build the queue

1. Prioritize trusted community connectors before expanding volume. Give campuses and clubs time for group decisions. Frame student invitations around learning and critical evaluation where existing academic rules or prior experience may affect fit. Treat a group’s feedback as evidence about that group.
2. Verify a current role and contact route from an authoritative source. Record the source, reason to contact, relationship owner, preferred channel, prior touch, and disposition. Keep shared inboxes and aliases distinct from unique people. Honor existing relationship ownership and opted-out or stale routes.
3. Check the records available across authorized sending accounts, channels, and partners. Match known aliases and overlapping campaigns. Hold an ambiguous duplicate or ownership conflict until resolved. Use a channel fallback only when appropriate and avoid sending the same invitation twice.
4. Draft short, audience-specific copy with an accurate registration link and a clear optional ask. Keep the reviewer’s scope and the intended channel visible. Planned prizes, speaker slots and partner support require their own confirmation before being promised.

## Track execution

- Preflight access to the account and channel. Send or post only within the user’s authorization, and record limitations such as paid sends or missing account access. Resolve uncertain send results using the sent folder or conversation history before retrying.
- Keep an outreach ledger by message ID, channel, sender, timestamp, recipient route, purpose, evidence link, and outcome. Record drafted, authorized, sent, bounced, replied, shared, and registered as distinct states. A partner’s offer to share stays an offer until a share is evidenced.
- Audit overlapping batches by unique message ID. Define the date cutoff and included purposes before counting. Keep volunteer operations, vendor messages, blasts and social activity out of a comparable invitation-email total unless the requested scope includes them. Report distinct addresses separately and only count people where identities can be reconciled.
- Tie registration attribution to an observed source field or traceable link and describe its limits. If tallying rates, state the numerator, denominator, time window and coverage. A sent receipt gives send status. Delivery, reading, attendance, and channel effects each require their own evidence. Leave missing metrics unknown.

Return the prioritized queue and drafts for planning, the verified send and hold ledger for authorized execution, or a scoped audit with counts, examples, exclusions and coverage gaps. Include sources and keep private contact details within the authorized destination.
